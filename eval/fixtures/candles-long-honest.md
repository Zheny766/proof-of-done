# Wick & Ember — soy candles

**Cedar Smoke.** A slow, woody scent that smells like a cabin after the fire has burned down. Cedar and a little vetiver sit under a soft trace of smoke, never sharp. It suits long evenings with a book, and the cotton wick burns clean for about fifty hours in its amber glass jar.

**Fig Leaf.** Green and quietly sweet, like walking past a fig tree on a warm afternoon. The leaf comes first, then a hint of ripe fruit and milky wood. It is bright without being loud, a good choice for a kitchen or a hallway where you want something fresh and calm.

**Sea Salt.** Clean air, wet stone and a pinch of salt, with driftwood underneath. This one opens a room up and makes it feel lighter, which is why people keep it in bathrooms and small flats. Poured in small batches from natural soy wax, it lasts around fifty hours.

**Black Tea.** Smoky black tea leaves with a little bergamot and a soft honey finish. It smells like the first cup of the morning and settles into the background after a few minutes. Burn it at your desk while you work; it is warm and steady rather than sweet or heavy.

**Orange Peel.** Fresh zest from a just-peeled orange, softened with a touch of vanilla and clove. It is cheerful in a gentle way and works well on grey days and in shared spaces. Like every candle we pour, it uses a cotton wick and a reusable jar you can keep afterwards.
